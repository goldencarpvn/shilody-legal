# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Shilody ("the app", "we", "us") is a music learning app provided by Nguyễn Đặng Thành Trung, an individual based in Vietnam. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Contact: glashion.studio@gmail.com

## 1. Who the app is for

Shilody is not intended for children under 16. Do not create an account if you are under 16. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 16, we will delete it.

## 2. Data we collect

**Account data**
- Email address and password (stored as a hash by our authentication provider), if you sign up with email.
- Name, email address and profile picture provided by Google, if you sign in with Google.
- A random user ID, if you use the app as a guest. Guest accounts do not require an email.
- Display name, only if you enter it in your profile. It is optional.

**Learning data**
- Your progress, answers, scores, statistics and settings (such as language and theme).

**Technical and diagnostic data**
- Crash reports and error logs, including device model, operating system version and app version (collected by Sentry). The app is configured not to send your IP address or user identity to Sentry.
- Usage analytics: screens you view and actions you take in the app, linked to your user ID, language and whether your account is a guest account (collected by PostHog).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone input. We do not use advertising SDKs and we do not sell your data.

## 3. How we use data

- To provide the app: sign you in, save your progress, show your statistics.
- To keep the app working: find and fix crashes and bugs.
- To improve the app: understand which features and lessons are used.
- To contact you about your account or a support request.

Legal bases (where GDPR applies): performance of the service you requested, our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and improving it, and your consent where we ask for it.

## 4. Who processes data for us

| Provider | Purpose | Data | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Authentication and database | Account data, learning data | Singapore |
| Google | Sign in with Google | Name, email, profile picture | Global |
| Sentry | Crash and error reporting | Diagnostic data (device, OS, app version, error details) | United States |
| PostHog | Product analytics | Usage events, user ID | United States |

These providers process data only to deliver their service to us. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own.

## 5. Retention

- Account and learning data: kept while your account exists.
- Diagnostic and analytics data: kept for the period set in our Sentry and PostHog settings, and removed from analytics when you delete your account (see section 6).
- When you delete your account, we delete your account and learning data from our database.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Edit**: you can change your display name in the app.
- **Delete your account**: in the app, open your account screen and choose "Delete account". This permanently deletes your account and learning data. If you cannot use the app, email us and we will do it for you.
- **Access, correction, export, objection**: email us. We respond within 30 days.
- Depending on where you live (for example Vietnam under its personal data protection law, the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights. We honor them on request.

## 7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with access controls on our database provider. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the date above and notify you in the app.
